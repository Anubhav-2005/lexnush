ABOUT LEXNUSH

Law with a Pulse.

Law doesn't exist in isolation. Neither should the way we read it.

LexNush was created from a simple belief: law is too important to remain inaccessible, and too alive to be treated as just text on a page.

A judgment is more than its final order. A Bill is more than its provisions. A legal development is more than a headline.

Behind every legal question are people, institutions, history, competing interests, and consequences.

That is where LexNush looks.

We explore the law not only as it is written, but as it moves through courts, Parliament, institutions, businesses, and everyday life.

Through Analysis, Law Explained, Judgment Explained, and From the Counsel's Desk, we bring together legal developments, context, commentary, and ideas that help readers understand what is happening, and why it matters.

OUR WORK ASKS FOUR SIMPLE QUESTIONS:

Our work asks four simple questions

  1. What happened?The development itself.
  2. Why did it happen?The law and context behind it.
  3. Why does it matter?Its impact beyond the immediate headline.
  4. What should we be thinking about next?The questions that remain after the judgment, Bill, or headline ends.

Because the law is not static.

It evolves.

It argues.

It responds.

It affects people.

It has a pulse.

THE PERSON BEHIND LEXNUSH

Law shouldn’t feel like a language spoken only by lawyers.

While studying law, Anushka Pandey found herself increasingly interested in the space beyond the text of a judgment, the questions behind it, the people affected by it, and the conversations it creates.

That curiosity became LexNush.

What began as a space to make legal information easier to understand has evolved into an independent legal publication exploring judgments, legislation, legal developments, ideas, and the stories shaping the law around us.

Through analysis, explainers, legal journalism, and thoughtful reflections, Anushka aims to make legal conversations clear without making them simplistic, analytical without making them inaccessible, and engaging without losing sight of accuracy.

Because the law does not exist only inside courtrooms or law reports. It moves through people, institutions, businesses, technology, and everyday life.

And that is what LexNush hopes to capture.
